Pariksha OMR

Photograph answer sheets, check them against your answer key, and send each student's marks to your Google Sheet.

Exam and marking

Blank and double-marked answers score 0. Questions without a key answer are skipped.

Answer key

Excel layout: question number in column A, answer letter in column B. A single column of letters also works.

Google Sheet link

One-time setup
  1. Open your Google Sheet, then Extensions, then Apps Script.
  2. Paste the contents of Code.gs, set your own passcode at the top, and save.
  3. Deploy, New deployment, type Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone. Copy the URL.
  4. Paste the URL and passcode above, then press Test connection.
  5. Optional: add a Students tab (registration number, name) to see names while scanning.
